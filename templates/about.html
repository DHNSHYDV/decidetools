About Decide Solutions

Purpose-built, clean online utilities engineered for speed, privacy, and simplicity.

Our Mission

Every day, millions of people search the web for essential file utilities: converting a resume from Word DOCX to PDF, generating a Wi-Fi or vCard QR code, or downloading a lecture for offline study. Too often, the search results lead to spammy websites loaded with deceptive download buttons, malware popups, intrusive trackers, or restrictive paywalls.

Decide Solutions was established to provide an open, transparent, and respectful alternative. We believe digital utilities should be lightning-fast, visually refined, and completely honest with users.

Privacy-First Architecture

We take a strict stance on data stewardship. We do not require accounts, we do not store documents, and we do not compile profiles of your online activity. Files processed through our API are written to isolated, ephemeral directories and wiped as soon as your download completes.

Open Engineering Standards

Our services run on proven open-source technologies: Python and Flask for high-performance concurrent request handling, FFmpeg for audio/video stream transcoding, Segno for mathematical QR vector compilation, and LibreOffice & pdf2docx for reliable document rendering.

Zero Clutter & Ad-Free Design

We reject deceptive pop-up banners and misleading "Download" links. The Decide Solutions user interface is focused on clean typography, responsive layout, dark-mode ergonomics, and direct action.

Continuous Reliability

Deployed on modern cloud infrastructure with automated health monitoring, Decide Solutions scales automatically to handle heavy conversion workloads without degrading speed or uptime.
