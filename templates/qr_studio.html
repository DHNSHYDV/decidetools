Free QR Code Generator Online

Quick Response (QR) codes are two-dimensional matrix barcodes that store encoded information such as web links, plain text notes, and wireless network configurations. The Decide Solutions Free QR Code Generator allows individuals, creators, and businesses to generate static QR codes instantly in their web browser without subscriptions, software installation, or watermarks.

How to Create a QR Code

  1. Choose Data Type: Select between standard Web Link & Text or Wi-Fi Network.
  2. Enter Your Content: Input your target URL, custom message, or wireless network credentials.
  3. Customize Style & Colors: Choose contrast colors for the modules and backdrop, and pick your preferred error correction level.
  4. Download: Click Export Raster (PNG) for immediate screen use or Export Scalable Vector (SVG) for physical print media.

QR Code Types

URL QR Codes

Direct users immediately to product pages, portfolio websites, digital menus, or landing pages when scanned with any modern iOS or Android camera app.

Text QR Codes

Encode plain text snippets, verification codes, instructions, or short messages that can be read offline without an active internet connection.

Wi-Fi QR Codes

Allow guests or customers to instantly connect to WPA, WPA2, or WPA3 wireless networks by scanning a code without ever typing complex passwords manually.

PNG vs SVG QR Codes

Choosing the right format ensures optimal scanning reliability:

  • PNG (Portable Network Graphics): A lossless raster format ideal for digital screens, mobile apps, social media, presentation slides, and standard desktop viewing.
  • SVG (Scalable Vector Graphics): A mathematical vector format composed of precise XML paths. SVG files can be enlarged to any dimension—including storefront banners, posters, and merchandise—with zero blurriness or pixelation.

QR Code Error Correction

QR codes incorporate Reed-Solomon error correction algorithms, allowing them to remain fully readable even if partially smudged, torn, or obstructed:

  • Level L (Low): Recovers up to 7% damaged data. Generates the simplest, least dense pattern.
  • Level M (Medium): Recovers up to 15% damaged data. The ideal default balance for general digital and screen use.
  • Level Q (Quartile): Recovers up to 25% damaged data. Recommended for industrial environments or marketing materials.
  • Level H (High): Recovers up to 30% damaged data. Best for physical print where wear, dirt, or branding overlays may obscure parts of the symbol.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create a QR code?

Select your desired payload type (URL, text, or Wi-Fi), enter your data in the input box, adjust colors or error correction if desired, and click 'Export Raster (PNG)' or 'Export Scalable Vector (SVG)' to download your QR code instantly.

Can I create a QR code for a website?

Yes. Choose the 'Web Link & Text' tab and paste your full website address including https://. When scanned by any smartphone camera, the browser will open the webpage immediately.

Can I create a Wi-Fi QR code?

Yes. Select the 'Wi-Fi Network' tab, input your Network SSID (name), password, and security encryption protocol (WPA/WPA2/WPA3 or Open). Scanning this QR code lets guests automatically join your wireless network without typing passwords manually.

Can I download my QR code as PNG?

Yes. The PNG export generates a high-resolution, pixel-crisp raster image that is perfect for web pages, emails, social media, and digital displays.

Can I download my QR code as SVG?

Yes. The SVG format is an infinite vector graphic. It can be scaled to any size—from business cards to massive outdoor billboards—without losing sharpness or pixelating.

Do QR codes expire?

No. Our generator creates static QR codes where your data is encoded directly into the pattern matrix. They do not redirect through external tracking servers, meaning they will work forever as long as your destination URL or Wi-Fi credentials remain valid.
