DOCX vs PDF

Understanding when to use each format improves your document workflows:

  • DOCX (Word): Best for active drafting, authoring, collaborating, and continuous revisions.
  • PDF (Portable Document Format): Best for archiving, printing, emailing, and official publishing where visual consistency is essential.
